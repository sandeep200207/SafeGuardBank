# 60-Second Judge Demo Script

## Opening

“Scammers do not need to break into a bank account. They only need to convince someone to send the money themselves. SafeGuard steps in before that payment completes, explains the warning signs in plain language, and brings in someone the customer trusts.”

## Demonstration

Choose Margaret. On Home, point out the safety score, the visible “Someone is asking me to pay right now” panic button, and the **One payment. Three layers of protection** panel.

Click **Rehearse the ₹45,000 demo flow**. Submit the pre-filled payment to “Officer James — Tax Dept” with the note “urgent penalty fee.” The application explains four warning signals: this is a new payee, the amount is unusual, the note uses urgency language, and the payment is late at night.

Answer “Yes” to the questions about a phone call and secrecy. The score escalates to blocked. Explain that the customer can cancel, wait, or bring in Priya rather than being shamed or forced into a technical decision.

Open a second tab as Priya. Show the pending approval and choose Approve or Block. Then open the analyst view to show the same intervention as bank-wide operational data.

## Closing

“SafeGuard is designed around a simple principle: pause first, explain clearly, and protect together.”
